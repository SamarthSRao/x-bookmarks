As always, Once you learn a concept, you start noticing it everywhere.

Worker pools and connection pools can be mixed and matched into some interesting backend design patterns. I discovered this while troubleshooting a bug I encountered years ago.

Each worker can have its own dedicated connection pool to a backend, or all workers can share one. Neither approach is universally better.. the tradeoffs depend on the use case.

I tell the story of how I discovered this concept in chapter 8 in my book, Root Cause, stories and lessons of two decades of backend engineering bugs, and expand on this further on Appendix A.
