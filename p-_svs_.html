Software used to be windmills. Now it's a motorcycle.

what do I mean by this? software used to be this slow grinding of the information space. humans have always been grinding the information space. software was just the last incantation.

this grinding, from the days of inventing/discovering language, writing, agriculture etc etc has taken different forms. These forms have lagged physical technology by a generation. You cant build transistors without an industrial machining base.

Software used to run in the background and grind away like a windmill. But the whole system has now become responsive. Now we get into our decks every morning and we're dynamically navigating.

the first to fall prey to this was social media. used to be a never thing where i was born. now its half life is one day. new elafda every day.

the same is happening to work. especially software development. most other work is basically walk into office and deal with whatever we find there. engineering is one of the few disciplines where this isn't true. no longer.

now your business has to react in realtime. this will not happen if you're riding a windmill. you will have to learn to ride the motorcycle.

software is now whatever data your agent can access easily.

new game in town. adapt or die.
