This is how I usually cold mail professors for research/internships:

Start with 2-3 lines on who you are and what you’ve worked on.

Then spend 2-3 lines connecting your work/interests to something they’re currently working on. Mention a paper/project of theirs if it genuinely overlaps with your interests, and explain why you’d like to work with them.

Then directly ask if they’d be open to having you as a research intern/collaborator.

End with something simple: “If this sounds relevant, I’d be happy to set up a quick call and discuss further.”

Keep it short, personalized, and make it obvious that you actually looked at their work.

Books I have followed/read for studying Core CS

1. Operating System : Operating System Concepts(by Galvin)
2. Networks : Andrew S. Tanenbaum - Computer Networks
3. Compiler Design : Principles of Compiler Design by Aho Ullman
4. Automata : An Introduction to Formal Languages and Automata by Peter Linz
5. Algorithms/DSA : CLRS (Cormen Book)
6. COA/Digital Logic : Computer organization and design : the hardware/software interface by Patterson
7. ML : CS229 Lecture Notes
8. Databases : Database System Concepts, by Korth
9. Math : Kreiznig (calculus), Sheldon Ross (probability and stats), Gilbert Strang (linear algebra)
