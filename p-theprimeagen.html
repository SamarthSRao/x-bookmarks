Most important question:
* are you excited about what you create and does it come through?

Sizing:
* what is the size of your niche?
* of your niche what % can even view the content
* example: you make vim content, but you are talking about a very specific problem in a very specific api. This will likely cause very few to even be able to engage with the content itself.
* How often do you break your niche and go broader audience? What does broader audience look like?

Simple Questions that can improve:
* what is your speaking cadence?
* how often do you side quest?
* do people feel connected to you or are you more clinical?
* how much time and effort do you put into thumbnails and titles?
* do your titles and thumbnails deliver on the promise of the content?
* people often make decisions within the first 30 seconds, what are you doing to make sure that the first 30 seconds people understand what they are getting into?
* is there a narrative that you are working towards? What is the story you are trying to tell? And does it come across?
